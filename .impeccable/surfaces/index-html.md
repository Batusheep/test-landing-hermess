# Surface brief — GetSmile landing

Scope: single-page landing, Persuade mode. Audience: patients 25–65 avoiding the dentist. Action: book a free check-up (phone or form).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a clinical daylight section — a calm white room where a warm sunbeam band marks the moment of action. It refuses the standard clinic template (centered hero + equal card grid) and the dark "premium medical" cliché.

OWN-WORLD: limewash white ground (#F4F6F5), section-cut charcoal (#232A2E) for text and the footer, a traveling warm gold beam (#F2B441) as the active band and primary action color, shadow blue (#8FA6B8) for secondary/withdrawn moments. Type: a clean grotesque display (Manrope, self-hosted via local files) over a readable body (system stack). One signature: the gold beam band that slides under headings and around the primary CTA, like sunlight crossing the room.

STORY: The visitor arrives afraid or skeptical; within one viewport they see the offer (free check-up), the proof (3D model before work, official Straumann, in-house diagnostics), and a single clear action. They leave with a phone number or a filled form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left 58% — headline "Стоматология, в которой не боятся" with the gold beam underlining the key phrase, subhead, primary CTA "Записаться на бесплатный осмотр" in gold, secondary phone link, 3 fact chips (Straumann partner, 3D + Trios, 9–21 daily). Right 42% — a drawn clinical section-diagram (SVG): a tooth cross-section with a light beam passing through, annotated with DSD/3D labels. No photo in the hero; the diagram is the proof.
